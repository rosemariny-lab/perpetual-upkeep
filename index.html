Departmental protocol

Drag chores between days and the Whenever column, or tap them to edit on mobile. The task colour shows who owns it; its dot shows the category. Fixed activities stay on the schedule. Recurring tasks create a fresh copy every Monday, while earlier weeks remain in your history. Big Clean tasks appear in the first full week of each month. This version saves only in this browser. Laundry chores may be deleted individually when not needed.

Laundry is simplified: optional wash loads and two flexible fold-and-put-away blocks start in Whenever. You can delete a laundry card from this week without affecting future weeks, or remove it permanently from the repeating template. Work blocks stay fixed.
